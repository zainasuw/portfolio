Dungeon Adventure

A 3D dungeon adventure you play in the browser. Pick a hero, wake the four pillars, beat the skeletons and escape through the golden door.

TypeScript · three.js · Vite

Play it on the portfolio Open the full game Code

Four heroes, one for every kind of player

Warrior, Priestess, Thief and Doctor, each with their own special ability. You choose with the mouse or the arrow keys, pick a difficulty, and your hero drops into the first hall.

  • Three difficulties, from kid-easy up.

Every hall has its own job

Follow the glowing gold marker. Light the lanterns, find the lost books, smash the crystals, catch the wisps or hold out. Then take the pillar at the altar and beat the skeletons it wakes.

  • Spend your coins at the merchant, pick a blessing, and walk through the golden door.
  • Wake all four pillars, then defeat the Skeleton King to escape.

Three ways to see it

  1. Through your hero’s eyesHow every run starts. The first hall is dark until you light the torch.
  2. Behind the heroPress V to step back and see who you are playing.
  3. From abovePress V again to look down on the whole hall.

How it’s built

  • Rendered with three.js, with ambient occlusion and post-processing for the candle-lit crypt.
  • 3D models compressed with meshopt through glTF-Transform, so the whole game loads in a few megabytes.
  • TypeScript and Vite, deployed to GitHub Pages by a GitHub Actions workflow on every push.

3D characters, dungeon pieces and animations: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (CC0). Ability icons: game-icons.net by Lorc, Delapouite, Darkzaitzev and Sbed (CC BY 3.0).
